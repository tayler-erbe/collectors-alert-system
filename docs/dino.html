Escaped Animals From Harbor Shipment Reported Roaming Scripps Ranch; Residents Told to Stay Indoors

Crew members aboard a cargo ship in San Diego Bay say several large animals broke out of crates bound for a zoo early Thursday. By mid-morning, residents in Scripps Ranch were reporting sightings near Lake Miramar.

By Roar Staff · Updated just now

SAN DIEGO: A shipment of live animals broke loose aboard the cargo vessel Ocean Odyssey shortly after sunrise Thursday as the ship was being unloaded near the downtown waterfront, according to crew members who spoke to the Roar.

Photos taken from the deck show several shipping crates splintered open and what appear to be large reptiles moving across the cargo area while workers in safety vests run toward the bridge. Crew members described the animals as "fast, and much bigger than anything on the manifest."

"We were told it was a standard live-cargo transfer," said one deckhand, who asked not to be named. "Then the crates started shaking. Everybody just ran."

Within the hour, drivers on Interstate 15 began calling in reports of large animals crossing the freeway near Miramar Road. By 9:30 a.m., residents in Scripps Ranch reported sightings near Lake Miramar and along Scripps Ranch Boulevard, where several said an animal "the size of a car" was feeding on landscaping.

"I heard something heavy in the yard and thought it was a delivery truck. Then I looked out the window. I've never seen anything like it."
— Scripps Ranch resident

Officials have issued a shelter-in-place advisory for Scripps Ranch and urged residents to keep children and pets indoors. Lake Miramar has been closed, and Scripps Ranch Boulevard is closed between Pomerado Road and Red Cedar Drive while crews work to contain the animals.

A spokesperson for the receiving facility said a specialist recovery team had been dispatched and that the animals "are not aggressive unless cornered," but asked the public not to approach them, feed them or attempt to take photos up close.

Shelter-in-place advisory: Scripps Ranch

  • Stay indoors and keep doors and gates closed.
  • Bring pets inside.
  • Do not approach, follow or feed any animal.
  • Report sightings to authorities; do not attempt to capture an animal.

It is not yet clear how many animals escaped or where the shipment originated. This is a developing story and will be updated.
